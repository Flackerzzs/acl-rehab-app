The graft is continuing its long remodelling process. Cells, blood supply and collagen structure continue to change over many months.

Calendar-based educational guide only. This cannot measure your graft's strength, incorporation or readiness. Your surgeon and physio guide progression.
What is happening biologically?

After ACL reconstruction, the tendon graft goes through overlapping stages of early healing, revascularisation and longer-term remodelling (often called ligamentisation). These stages vary between people and graft types. Research describes ongoing changes beyond 12 months, sometimes extending longer.

This app estimates the broad phase from your surgery date only. It cannot tell how strong your graft is, whether it has fully incorporated into bone, or whether you are ready to run, jump or return to sport.
